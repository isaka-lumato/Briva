Who We Are

Bridging Geological Discovery and Responsible Production

Briva Exploration & Mining is a Dar es Salaam–headquartered consultancy uniting experienced field geologists, geophysicists, mining engineers, financial analysts, and corporate advisors. Together, our leadership brings more than two decades of hands-on operational experience across East Africa, West Africa, and South Asia.

We serve international exploration companies, institutional partners, industrial mineral producers, and Tanzanian Primary Mining Licence (PML) cooperatives—providing accurate subsurface data, practical engineering designs, and transparent transaction advisory.

Our Vision

To be the leading mining and exploration solutions provider in Africa, recognized for innovation, sustainability, and uncompromising integrity.

Our Mission

To deliver end-to-end, reliable, and cost-effective mining and exploration services through technical excellence, environmental stewardship, and strong community engagement.

Commodity Experience

Operating Principles

Six Core Values Governing Every Engagement

Meet Our Leadership Team

01. Uncompromising Integrity
