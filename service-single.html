Overview & Technical Scope

Briva delivers disciplined, data-driven mineral exploration programmes designed to de-risk prospecting licences and advance high-priority targets toward resource definition. Our geologists combine deep knowledge of East African greenstone belts, pegmatite fields, coal basins, and metamorphic terrains with rigorous QA/QC protocols.

From desktop GIS compilation and remote-sensing interpretation through systematic grid sampling, trenching, and drill target prioritization, every campaign is structured to generate defensible geological datasets ready for investor review.

Key Technical Deliverables

Desktop geological review & historical data compilation
1:5,000 to 1:25,000 litho-structural outcrop & regolith maps
Soil, stream sediment, rock-chip & channel sampling with QA/QC
Ground geophysical survey planning & anomaly interpretation
Drill-ready target ranking & phased exploration budgets
Statutory technical exploration reports for licensing compliance

Execution Methodology

STEP 01

Desktop Synthesis & Target Framing

Integration of regional aeromagnetics, satellite imagery, and historical tenure data to prioritize prospective corridors.

STEP 02

Systematic Field Mapping & Geochemistry

Traversing, structural logging, trenching, and multi-element sampling governed by strict certified reference material (CRM) insertion.

STEP 03

Anomaly Integration & Drill Targeting

3D structural synthesis of surface geochemistry and geophysics to site high-confidence scout and infill drill collars.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which mineral commodities has Briva explored in Tanzania?

Our field teams have executed exploration and prospecting programmes for gold, lithium-bearing pegmatites, beryllium, flake graphite, copper-zinc base metals, coal, dolomite, and industrial quartz across Dodoma, Morogoro, Mpanda, Merelani, Tabora, and Namtumbo.

How do you ensure geochemical sample integrity and QA/QC?

Every sampling batch incorporates chain-of-custody documentation, field duplicates, blanks, and certified reference standards prior to submission to accredited laboratories.

Can Briva evaluate an early-stage Prospecting Licence (PL) before acquisition?

Yes. We conduct rapid technical due-diligence site visits, verification sampling, and geological prospectivity assessments for investors evaluating Tanzanian mineral tenures.
